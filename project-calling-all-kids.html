Calling All Kids: turning routines into playful character calls

Calling All Kids LLC wanted brushing teeth, cleaning up and bedtime to feel like play instead of a fight. We built a speech-recognition powered app that makes it happen, live on the App Store and Google Play.

4.9 ★ App Store Rating 64 Ratings iOS & Android Ages 4+
The Challenge

Calling All Kids LLC needed a way to make daily parenting routines, like brushing teeth, cleaning up or getting ready for bed, feel like play rather than a battle, for children aged 2 to 8.

Our Approach

We built speech-recognition-powered "phone calls" from favourite characters across 30+ themes, with parent-curated content controls and safety-first data handling built in from the start, shipped for both iOS and Android.
