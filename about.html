TechMonx is a technology studio built around one idea: local accountability, delivered at a standard that holds up anywhere in the world.

Who We Are

Turning creative ideas into digital realities

Founded in Glasgow, TechMonx partners with startups, SMEs and enterprises across the UK, Europe and North America. You get a Glasgow-based team you can call or meet in person, backed by engineering and security cover around the clock.

Whether it's a new app, a CRM your sales team will actually use, a stronger IT setup, or better protection against cyber threats, we work alongside your team rather than as an outside vendor you rarely hear from.

Evolving through craft, care and delivery
