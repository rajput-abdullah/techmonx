Who We Are

From code to customers, built to scale

TechMonx is a Glasgow-based technology studio, founded in January 2026, building AI agents, apps, websites, CRMs and SaaS platforms, backed by practical IT support and cybersecurity. We help ambitious businesses turn ideas into reliable digital products.

Our expertise spans AI automation, web development, mobile app development, UI/UX design, DevOps, game development, blockchain, and quality assurance. We work with modern technologies including React, Next.js, Swift, Flutter, AWS, and TensorFlow.

The consumer apps in our portfolio, including FaceBloom, Calling All Kids and Church Service Hub, were built by our founder, Abdullah Shahbaz, an AI Agentic Software Engineer specialising in AI-based Flutter applications, before TechMonx was founded, and are now part of the studio's portfolio. Agile delivery, NDA and IP protection, direct communication, and post-launch support on every project.

Our Story

A founder's craft, built into a studio

TechMonx was founded in January 2026 by Abdullah Shahbaz and Sami Khan. The portfolio you see across our site, AI-powered Flutter apps, web platforms and business tools, reflects work Abdullah completed as an independent AI Agentic Software Engineer before TechMonx existed. We built the studio to take that same standard of engineering and offer it more broadly, alongside IT support, cybersecurity, SaaS and CRM development.
