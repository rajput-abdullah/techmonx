TechMonx Leads — Google Sheet webhook (optional CRM layer)
============================================================

Your website already captures every lead (contact form, chat widget,
booking modal), tags it, logs it to a protected file on the server, and
sends your team an instant email alert plus the visitor an introductory
auto-reply — all of that already works with no setup.

This file adds an OPTIONAL extra step: every new lead also gets appended
as a row to a Google Sheet you control, so you have a shareable, filterable
lead list without needing to log into the server. Takes about 3 minutes.

STEP 1 — Create the sheet
--------------------------
1. Go to https://sheets.google.com and create a new blank spreadsheet.
2. Rename it "TechMonx Leads" (click the title top-left).
3. In row 1, add these column headers (A1:L1):
   Timestamp | Form Type | Name | Email | Phone | Company | Service | Budget | Timeline | Tags | Details | Notes

STEP 2 — Add the script
------------------------
1. In the sheet, go to Extensions > Apps Script.
2. Delete anything in the editor and paste the code below.
3. Click the disk/Save icon, name the project "TechMonx Leads Webhook".

--- PASTE BELOW THIS LINE INTO THE APPS SCRIPT EDITOR ---

function doPost(e) {
  var sheet = SpreadsheetApp.getActiveSpreadsheet().getSheetByName('Sheet1')
    || SpreadsheetApp.getActiveSpreadsheet().getActiveSheet();
  var data = JSON.parse(e.postData.contents);

  var tags = Array.isArray(data.tags) ? data.tags.join(', ') : '';

  sheet.appendRow([
    data.timestamp || new Date().toISOString(),
    data.form_type || '',
    data.name || '',
    data.email || '',
    data.phone || '',
    data.company || '',
    data.service_label || data.service || '',
    data.budget || '',
    data.timeline || '',
    tags,
    data.details || '',
    data.notes || data.message || ''
  ]);

  return ContentService.createTextOutput(JSON.stringify({ ok: true }))
    .setMimeType(ContentService.MimeType.JSON);
}

--- PASTE ABOVE THIS LINE ---

STEP 3 — Deploy as a Web App
------------------------------
1. Click "Deploy" (top right) > "New deployment".
2. Click the gear icon next to "Select type" and choose "Web app".
3. Description: "TechMonx leads webhook".
4. "Execute as": Me (your Google account).
5. "Who has access": Anyone.
   (This does NOT make your sheet public — it only allows this one
   script endpoint to receive POST requests. Nobody can read your sheet
   through this URL, they can only submit a new row to it, and only your
   own website will know the URL.)
6. Click "Deploy". Google will ask you to authorize the script the first
   time — this is your own script running under your own account, so
   review and click "Allow".
7. Copy the "Web app URL" it gives you (ends in /exec).

STEP 4 — Connect it to your website
--------------------------------------
Send me that URL, or paste it yourself into contact-handler.php:

  $crmWebhookUrl = 'PASTE_YOUR_URL_HERE';

(near the top of the file, currently set to an empty string). Once that's
set and pushed live, every new lead will also land in this sheet within a
second or two of the visitor submitting the form.

That's it — no ongoing cost, no third-party account, and you can always
swap this out for a real CRM (HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive) later by pointing
$crmWebhookUrl at that platform's incoming-webhook URL instead.
