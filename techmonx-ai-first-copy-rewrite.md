## 2. Flagship Offering: Productised Entry Offer

**Section title:**
> **The AI Automation & Feasibility Audit**
> *A fixed-fee, two-week diagnostic that tells you exactly where AI can save you money, before you commit to a build.*

**What's included (bullets):**

- **Operational bottleneck mapping**: we trace your sales, support and ops workflows end to end and flag exactly where time and money are leaking.
- **Quantified ROI projection**: a cost savings and hours reclaimed model, built against your actual numbers, not industry averages.
- **Custom AI agent blueprint**: a build ready architecture for your top automation opportunities, scoped and ready to hand to any engineering team.
- **Executive readout**: a written report plus a walkthrough call. Yours to keep, whether or not you build it with us.

**CTA button copy:**
> **Claim Your Audit Slot →**

*(Alternate, if you want more urgency: "Book Your Audit: Limited Slots This Quarter")*

Positioning note: this sits **above** your existing Starter/Growth/Pro automation packages as the lowest friction entry point, a paid diagnostic rather than a sales call, which is what makes it credible to mid-market buyers who are wary of scope creep.
