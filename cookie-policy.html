Cookie Policy

What this site stores in your browser, and how to control it.

Last updated: 27 July 2026

This page explains what cookies and similar browser storage techmonx.co.uk uses, why, how long each is kept, and how you can manage or withdraw your consent at any time.

Strictly necessary storage

This category is always on and cannot be switched off, because the site cannot function properly without it. It does not require consent under UK GDPR / PECR.

NamePurposeRetention
techmonx_cookie_consent_v1Remembers your cookie choice (accepted / rejected analytics) so we don't ask again every visit.Until you clear browser storage or change your choice

Analytics cookies (only with your consent)

If you accept analytics cookies via the banner or the "Cookie Settings" link, we load Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to help us understand how visitors use the site — which pages are popular, how people navigate, and roughly how many visitors we get. GA4 is not loaded, and no analytics cookie is set, until you opt in.

NamePurposeRetention
_gaDistinguishes unique visitors for Google Analytics 4.13 months
_ga_<container-id>Persists session state for Google Analytics 4.13 months

Event and user-level data collected by GA4 itself (as opposed to the browser cookie) is retained in Google's systems for 14 months, after which it is automatically deleted, per GA4's default retention setting. We do not extend this. We use IP anonymisation and disable Google Signals/ads personalisation on our GA4 property, and we do not send names, email addresses, message content, or any other personal data as analytics events — see "What we track" below.

What we track

When analytics is active, we record generic, non-identifying interactions: page views, which service or portfolio/project pages are viewed, clicks on "Start a Project" and "Let's Talk" buttons, opens of the booking widget and completed booking requests, contact-form and AI-assistant chat submissions, phone-number and email-address clicks, and clicks to our social media profiles. None of these events include the content of what you typed, your email address, phone number, or any other personal or sensitive information.

Legal basis and consent

Under UK GDPR and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), we only set analytics cookies with your prior, informed, opt-in consent. Rejecting analytics, or simply closing the banner, means analytics cookies are not set and GA4 is not loaded — the site otherwise works exactly the same either way.

Managing or withdrawing your consent

You can change your mind at any time by clicking "Cookie Settings" in the footer of any page, which reopens the preferences panel so you can switch analytics on or off. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser's own settings. Blocking cookies may affect some websites' functionality generally, but will not affect your ability to browse this site or contact us.

Other policies

For details on what personal data we collect through our forms, why, and your rights, see our Privacy Policy.
