FAQ

Common questions

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope, a focused MVP or single platform build usually runs 6–12 weeks, while larger multi-platform products or ongoing SaaS builds run longer. We give you a realistic timeline estimate after an initial scoping call, before any commitment.

How is pricing worked out?

Most projects are quoted as a fixed price against an agreed scope, based on complexity, platforms, and integrations. For ongoing work or evolving products, we can also work on a monthly retainer. You'll always get a clear quote before work starts, no surprise invoices.

What technologies do you use?

We choose the stack to fit the project rather than forcing one framework on everything, common choices include React/Next.js and native mobile frameworks for frontend, Node.js/Python for backend, Flutter for cross-platform apps, and Unity for games. We're happy to work within an existing codebase or stack if you already have one.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes a post-launch support window to fix any issues that come up, and most clients move onto an ongoing maintenance or support retainer afterwards. Active clients also get 24/7 support access.

Who owns the code and IP once the project is finished?

You do. Once a project is paid in full, full ownership of the source code, design assets and any related IP transfers to you. We don't retain rights to reuse your proprietary code or designs elsewhere.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, we're happy to sign an NDA before any detailed scoping discussion if you'd like one in place, just let us know when you get in touch.

Can you handle App Store / Play Store publishing?

Yes, we handle the end-to-end submission process for both the Apple App Store and Google Play Store, including store listing assets, compliance requirements, and review-process troubleshooting, so your app goes live without you having to navigate that process yourself.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us what you're trying to solve and we'll recommend the right starting point, no obligation.
