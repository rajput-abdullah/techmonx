Careers

Build your career in Glasgow, work on projects everywhere

We're a small, accountable team that ships real products for real clients. If you'd rather do focused, craft-driven work than sit in a huge org chart, you'll probably like it here.

Life at TechMonx

Small team, real ownership

Every person here works directly on client-facing products, not internal tooling nobody sees. You'll ship features that end up on the App Store, in production SaaS platforms, and in front of real users within weeks, not quarters.

We're Glasgow-based and proud of it, but our clients span the UK, US and EU, so you get local working culture with genuinely international project exposure.

A team of specialists Engineering, design, DevOps, QA and client success under one roof
How We Work

The values behind every project

These aren't posters on a wall — they're how decisions actually get made day to day.

Ownership & Craft

You own your work end to end, from first commit to production, and it shows in the quality bar we hold each other to.

Direct Communication

No layers of management between you and the decision. If something's not working, you can say so and it gets fixed.

Growth & Mentorship

Working across app, web, AI, blockchain and QA projects means you're never doing the same thing on repeat for years.
