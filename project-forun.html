Forun: everyday services, brought into one app

Forun is an all-in-one super-app for rides, food delivery, courier services, car rentals and gifts, bringing everyday services together in one convenient platform for both riders and senders.

Built by Abdullah Shahbaz, TechMonx's founder, before the studio's founding in January 2026; now part of the TechMonx portfolio.

App · Super App iOS & Android
The Challenge

Forun set out to replace a handful of separate apps, one for rides, one for food, one for parcels, with a single, reliable platform that feels consistent from booking a ride to sending a gift abroad.

Our Approach

We contributed to a unified super-app experience spanning ride hailing, food delivery, courier services, car rentals and gifting, all sharing one account, one wallet and one support experience.
